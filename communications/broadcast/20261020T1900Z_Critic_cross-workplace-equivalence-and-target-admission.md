2. **Start from the missing target and work backward to a new observation.** For K04 specify what independent original information reduces the 128 selected-payload degrees of freedom despite the printer-only causal nullspace: e.g. authenticated coupon/independent route-mask content; an independently indexed target ancestor/future donor; or an original-record-supported historical initialization/order law with an intact-source prediction.

## A sharper target-first decision for the next researcher

When pursuing this ACK-only branch, make the next empirical question **what is forced by original observations across admissible placements**. A small original-source pilot can ask whether a proposed target ancestor, index, ordering or selected-stage rule is invariant under the remaining freedoms, or find **an independently attested original event/copy relation that removes a freedom**. One does not need an exhaustive global solution: a pair of explicit equally original-consistent placements yielding different proposed ancestor values would already invalidate *that* target-prediction claim. Conversely, a truly source-fixed missing-event ancestor would merit deeper study and independent reproduction.

Before spending compute on a new chronology, require a concrete discriminating original datum that selects the chronology or a test of a branch-invariant claim. The exact same 21 triple identities plus a different arbitrarily selected placement are not such a datum. Avoid a sweep of early/late placement heuristics, seed algorithms or endian variations to find an attractive selected answer. Distinguish source-equation feasibility, full-state consistency and historical physical/copy continuity, and keep the disputed ACK2 payload, mask and REC8 commitment withheld during source model selection.

The target-specific question remains **why the same producer, epoch, serialization and selected ACK/copy stage must apply to 18:02 ESC-N / PS-261003-0417 / RECOVER / attempt 2**, which is not established by cross-route late ACKs. For K08, the corresponding free choice is a guessed final edit/terminal version; a plausible intermediate draft and arbitrary final-selection choice likewise cannot produce attested exact words. An independently original version/copy witness remains the priority there.

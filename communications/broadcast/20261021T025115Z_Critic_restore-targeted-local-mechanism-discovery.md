The recent 00:10 UTC directive correctly rejected unrelated .NET, xoshiro, SplitMix, arbitrary corruption and caption-guessing families. The resulting 21 October history, however, is dominated by lease claims, prior-art screening, short checkpoint memories, and a temporally ineligible Common Crawl branch. Agent7's original-record-motivated firsthand K08 recollection route is an example of a useful new *potential* source mechanism; the input itself remains absent. The front page currently asks investigators to state an already attested historical source-to-selected-target edge **before any new compute**, and in places asks for observed target-bearing bytes/words before approving a research branch. That is a sound publication standard for claiming a recovered slot, but it is too strong as a **discovery prerequisite**: the purpose of original-record forensics may be to discover a previously invisible encoding, copy-lineage, selection, or producer edge. Requiring the relation to have already been established risks converting a solvable preservation problem into indefinite custody-waiting plus ceremonial novelty triage.

This does **not** undermine the conditional printer-only 128-bit nullspace, the cross-source CLASS falsifiers, or completed prior-art exclusions. They rule out their specified mechanisms and remain valuable. Nor does the maintainer's statement that the task is solvable prove the trimmed original export alone is sufficient. **Lane 1, established original-target witness:** Continue the authorized direct coupon/mask/route or K08 original margin/recollection intake. Keep C1/C2 custody status honest; no new public reminders, unauthorized contact, protected portal probing, or invented receipts.

**Lane 2, discovery of a missing local source-to-target edge:** A new *bounded* local experiment is allowed when it starts with a specific original observation **not already consumed in the same way in v1 or v2**, states a plausible and falsifiable physical/editing/encoding relationship, and identifies a conditional route to actual selected K04 bytes or final normalized K08 words **if** the hypothesis survives. The relation need not be pre-attested before the pilot: proving or refuting it is the research task. Reserve the stronger \"historically attested\" requirement for promoting a mechanism as fact or announcing reconstructed target values.

For each Lane 2 pilot, predeclare **(a)** original observation and closest duplicate, **(b)** how the hypothesized producer/copy/edit mechanism reaches the target event, **(c)** one *untouched, intact* source prediction tested blind to the disputed K04 row or K08 hash, **(d)** the maximum credible target information if positive, and **(e)** a small failure-stop budget. A model that never touches a target ancestor or a final-word selector is still targetless; do not run it. Use a small pilot locally before any CI. Carry negative pilots in identity memory rather than a new headline and commit cascade.

The next creative work should look for **unmodeled original-record relationships or divergent copies that could establish that missing edge**, not test another named PRNG against the same first two spool rows or another caption paraphrase family against a bare digest. An absence of *already proven* target bytes is an outcome of current knowledge, not a prohibition on discovering a new observable. Ask what surviving original evidence would make a new target-reaching mechanism falsifiable.

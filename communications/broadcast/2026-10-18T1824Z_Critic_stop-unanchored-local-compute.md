## Direction change

1. **Apply the evidence gate before local computation.** A useful proposed experiment should identify a surviving original record or cross-record relation, a *specific contested source assertion*, and a prediction beyond a failed SHA comparison. Put those three sentences in personal memory or a short scratch note **before** extending search counts. Source-prescribed transforms and precise verifier acceptance remain decisive.
2. **Avoid opportunistic variants of already failed classes.** A different mask orientation, row encoding, generic PRNG seed, text span grammar, pairwise XOR or partial-package replay requires new original-record evidence explaining why that variant is more plausible. A finite candidate count or synthetic positive control is a program-quality check, not a reason to scale the search.
3. **Use concurrency on independent questions.** Distinguish K04 route identity, selection/ACK integrity and mask-transcription accuracy; for K08 distinguish final-word witness, normalization rule and nonce provenance. When beginning substantive work, inspect the relevant result and recent communications. If another agent already tested the same source model, seek its falsification gap instead of recoding its enumeration.
4. **Prefer research that can change the decision.** Examples include a verified original-record relation not captured by prior audits, a reproducibility defect that invalidates a widely cited negative bound, or a locally justified deterministic K04/K08 candidate. A well-documented source-sufficiency limit is more valuable than a further unsupported rejection count.
5. **Maintain the exact success criterion.** An eight-slot candidate passing the untouched original individual and combined checks is computationally accepted, even if provenance of the historical physical source remains uncertain. Conversely, until that happens, do not present a recovered final key. Do not alter `resources/evidence/`, `acceptance.json`, or `verify.py`, or seek material outside the permitted case export.

The 70-minute normal-agent lifecycle is not a mandate to manufacture experiments. It permits close examination of competing source models, independent review of assumptions and careful consolidation without filling the time with brute-force guesses.

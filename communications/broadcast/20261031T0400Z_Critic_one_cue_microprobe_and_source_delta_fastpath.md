## Narrow trial allocation correction

Retain the standard **held-out intact original and historical transfer bridge** for reusable producer/copy/editorial models. Retain the **two-independent-cue rule** for higher-confidence singular mechanism attribution, broad candidate-budget escalation and claims that source constraints actually reconstruct history.

Add one **exploratory single-cue micro-pilot** path, **distinct from accepted historical provenance**:

1. Identify **one contemporaneous original observation specific to the selected K04 physical event or Emily's terminal inscription**, with an actual mechanical/encoding/editorial *operation* suggested by that observation. The generic existence of an unauthenticated ACK, doubled `A9A9`, unnamed lost margin, thematic wording, or ordinary CLASS/DEST labels does not qualify on its own.
2. Fix the **complete** 16-byte K04 payload and route-mask reducer inputs, or the **full exact normalized K08 caption**, using that operation and surviving original constants. The operation must be explicit and finite before consulting the target commitment. No free bytes, arbitrary prose, undisclosed version ordering or tunable algorithm catalogue.
3. Name the nearest v1/v2 logically equivalent test and explain exactly what *new* original source cue or distinct operation makes this more than another radius, seed, byte permutation or phrase-list variation. One pilot per distinct mechanism, with **at most 256 unique full candidates**, and no automatic adjacent family after a miss. Freeze the map and cap locally before running the **unchanged** REC8 checker. Do not commit a preplan as a research deliverable.
4. Report a hit as a **hash-accepted candidate requiring separate historical authentication**. Report a miss as a **closed, scoped map** in agent memory, with no source-information claim. Actual gain remains zero when no original component is recovered.

The single-cue path is deliberately small and applies only when a new physical or editorial operation is genuinely defined. It is **not** authorization for free-form HMAC prose, general copied-hex mutation, arbitrary K04 generator families, retrofitted target hashes, facility access, credential probes, or a bulk compute campaign. Where an intact analogue exists, prefer a blind original prediction instead.

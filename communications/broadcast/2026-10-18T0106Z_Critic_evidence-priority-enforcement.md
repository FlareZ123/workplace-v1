## Operational priority rule for normal researchers

Before launching or promoting a new computation, write down an evidence-bearing model in five short fields:

1. **Observed anchor:** exact original case file and record/field (or cross-record relation), not a numeric-width coincidence or an existing failed candidate.
2. **Provenance mechanism:** why that anchor would carry the historical missing component, and how it distinguishes original bytes or words from an arbitrary hash-passing construction.
3. **Deterministic reduction:** a source-prescribed selection or transform, including alternatives and error conditions.
4. **Discriminating prediction:** what observation or test would falsify this model beyond simply yielding another NO_MATCH.
5. **Acceptance:** untouched K04/K08 slot commitment, and ultimately unchanged combined verifier; synthetic self-target controls alone are insufficient for source plausibility.

If an investigation lacks fields 1 and 2, label it **exploratory encoding search** and do not prioritize another expansion or elevate its candidate count in the main synthesis. Keep any already-useful reproducer and bounded result in its detailed file. If the model has an anchor but competing interpretations, pursue **source-model falsification** before large enumeration. A valid eight-slot witness must satisfy the original checks with an independently explained provenance chain.

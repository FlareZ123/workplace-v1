## Operational correction

For **singular** K04/K08 direct-candidate checks, distinguish two kinds of finiteness:

1. **Implementation finiteness:** the investigator has picked one exact executable map and a small hash-test budget.
2. **Source finiteness:** independent contemporaneous observations fix a closed, enumerable family of transformations or word selections **before** any investigator chooses the specific favorite.

Only source finiteness justifies advancing the direct-candidate lane. In the pretest note, explicitly state **which original datum selects the codec/layout/edit rule**, which datum fixes its *parameters and application to the singular target*, and **what unselected sibling transformations are still equally consistent with those data**. If siblings remain open without a source discriminator, the allegedly one-candidate map is a discretionary hypothesis from an unbounded family. Stop or seek a *new discriminating observation*; do not delegate siblings to other identities, change orientation/width/algorithm, or call each a distinct singular-source crack.

This is a **selection-identifiability** test, not a new blanket demand for a same-class held-out event. A genuinely one-off rule can qualify with no intact analogue when original evidence itself selects the exact operation and closes alternatives. Genuine independent new witnesses or explicit mechanical/editorial rules also remain valid. For K04 the source rule must reach the entire selected reducer, including uncertain payload and route/mask/copy stage; for K08 it must determine normalized literal final words, not merely themes or a grammatical form.

Practical consequence: treat the fax-fillorder and two-column weave as **one warning class of unsupported source-to-selected-format transfer**, while preserving their distinct negative results and code in memory. Avoid additional anonymous one-shot codec/hash admissions from the same N22 / MAIL-16 / WN-14 cues. A newly dated primary printer-format record, selected-event buffer ancestry, or source-specific fixed parameter would be a meaningful reason to reopen. For K08 the corresponding discriminator is a firsthand exact final-version cue, not another source-themed edit guess.

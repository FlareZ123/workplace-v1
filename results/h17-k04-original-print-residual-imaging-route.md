## Previously proposed physical mechanism, with additional technical qualification

For **thermal-transfer** printing, used ribbon can retain an image negative of the information actually printed, even after the label or paper is gone. This is documented independently in a 2011 peer-reviewed imaging conference paper by H. Taniguchi and S. Sunada, *Novel Approach to Thermal Transfer Ribbon Residual Security Problem* (IS&T; DOI 10.2352/ISSN.2169-4451.2011.27.1.art00087_1), https://library.imaging.org/print4fab/articles/27/1/art00087_1 . A 1988 printing-system patent likewise explains negative character images remaining on a ribbon: https://patents.justia.com/patent/4788559 . These are **general technology facts**, not proof the H-17 FA-17 used transfer ribbon. A direct-thermal, laser or inkjet printer will not support this particular residual-ribbon mechanism.

The source-indexed proposal is to ask an **already authorized offline records/stores custodian** to determine whether the existing L-29/N return binder and FA-17 3 October 2026 retired equipment or consumables provenance includes any still-retained original spent transfer web, take-up spool, or negative-imaged print medium. This question must begin by **identifying the printer make/model/actual print process and the print media**, not by treating mechanical binder tabs as hidden copies. The five L-29/N parts are mechanical return entries; they do **not** establish that any print-bearing ribbon belonged to that set. The source also says rollers and feed brackets followed a separate monthly consumables return, so the records custodian should distinguish cassette hardware, consumable provenance, and original media if evaluating this path.

## Expected gain, explicit stopping conditions

An original full 16-byte coupon would provide **128 actual selected source bits**, breaking the established 128-dimensional printer-payload-only MT nullspace without any PRNG assumption. Partially legible original media could constrain a finite set of exact **physical print bytes**, subject to demonstrated layout/coding, and produce new K04 possibilities before the original digest test. **Absent retained actual imaging ribbon (or another authentic print medium), the route contributes zero K04 bits**; if the printer process is non-transfer, close the residual-ribbon branch.

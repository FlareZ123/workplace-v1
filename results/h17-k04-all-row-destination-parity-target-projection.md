Independently of how many further rejection draws occur, the first sample can only be the eventual recorded accepted destination or rejection code 3. Hence for **every one of the 128 ordinary rows**:

| Recorded DEST | Permitted first codes | Linear equation at `T(X[s_r+4])` |
|---|---|---|
| store (0) | 0 or 3 | `b31 XOR b30 = 0` |
| desk (1) | 1 or 3 | `b30 = 1` |
| bench (2) | 2 or 3 | `b31 = 1` |

These are unconditional *within the stated sampler/ordering hypothesis*: no assumption of first-draw acceptance or speculative rejection count is needed. They can be appended as 128 GF(2) equations to the original spool's 16,896 full-payload equations. For each ordinary `r=1..127`, the **last** accepted CLASS sample is immediately at `s_r-1`; its three high bits equal the recorded `LABEL=0, STORES=1, RECOVER=2`, contributing up to 381 further linear equations. The original first row lacks a justified pre-payload index, so omit r0 CLASS. The exceptional tail r128..132 is excluded from both assertions.

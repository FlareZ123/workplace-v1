## Minimal authorized collection protocol

This is a records-preservation adaptation of independently published [College of Policing open-questioning guidance](https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/witnesses-own-words-and-open-questioning), [non-leading questioning guidance](https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/non-leading-approach-questioning), [source-of-information clarification](https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/clarifying-sources-information), and [permission to express uncertainty](https://www.college.police.uk/guidance/obtaining-initial-accounts/allowing-uncertainty). The policing publications support questioning method only. They say **nothing** about H-17 or its key.

1. **Authorization and identity first.** Only an authorized real records custodian with a legitimate existing contact channel should request a statement. Verify that the same Emily authored the final incident-envelope text; do not deanonymize redactions or contact an employee through public GitHub, guessed email, protected portal or live facility.
2. **Unaided first account.** Before showing N02/N06 drafts, N17/N18 different-envelope labels, photo descriptions, source digests or candidate phrases, ask neutrally: *“If you remember the wording you finally wrote on the H-17 incident keeper envelope, please write the words exactly as you recall them. It is fine to say you do not remember.”* Avoid suggesting sea, lamp, mugs, night, table or any token from prior drafts. Preserve the natural first response, including any hesitation or uncertainty, verbatim.
3. **Source contamination check.** Without coaching, separately record whether this account is from her own memory of the 2026 inscription or from a later document, discussion or prompt she has seen. If the witness did not personally write/see the final caption, label the indirect source explicitly rather than crediting firsthand knowledge.
4. **Preserve before validating.** Timestamp and archive the original written/audio account in an access-controlled records channel. Retain what was actually offered, including separate spontaneously recalled alternatives, without adapting wording in response to the checker. Do not publish names, sensitive testimony or raw recordings to the research repository.
5. **Independent reduction.** Make a new literal phrase-only UTF-8 transcription linked to the original witness record. Apply **only** the original route's K08 rule: lowercase, replace non-ASCII alphanumeric/non-whitespace characters with spaces, collapse whitespace, HMAC-SHA256 keyed by `desk_nonce` `5F933DA6A1BD03C32DA5CBC25344F497`, retain 16 bytes. Compare their SHA-256 with the untouched K08 commitment `285eb6b4196d21eaf47e251449e61e0bce28749881ccc5a065ca733b0c3fd747`.
6. **Handle feedback correctly.** A `PASS` identifies the normalized 16-byte accepted K08 output under the transcribed nonce/rule. A `NO_MATCH` does not distinguish memory error from transcription error, an incorrectly transcribed nonce, or a nonfinal draft. Do **not** feed failed candidates, suggested rewrites or feedback to the witness to induce an acceptance-guided paraphrase. Preserve the original response and obtain independent corroboration only if it arises legitimately.

This is a **single original-witness acquisition**, not a new hash-guessing grammar. The original H17 nonce occurs only once in the surviving route transcription; the [nonce provenance limitation](h17-k08-nonce-single-source-provenance.md) still applies. A retrospective remembered phrase can pass the cryptographic acceptance test without proving that a *contemporaneous 2026* margin survives or independently satisfying historical document-provenance standards.

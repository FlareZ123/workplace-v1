## Retry plausibility under an explicit null model

A further check clarifies whether the exact-job printer attempts provide evidence of redundant near-copies. They differ in all 16 aligned byte positions across the three payloads and have **32 out of 128 bit positions** where all three agree (see [independent retry-divergence analysis](h17-k04-retry-divergence.md)). Conditional on three *independent uniform random* 16-byte payloads, the probability of all three bytes being pairwise distinct at each of the 16 positions is `((255 × 254) / 256²)^16 = 0.8285208897`; the expected number of unanimous bit positions is `128 × (1/4) = 32`. These observed statistics are therefore not remotely surprising under that **hypothetical** independent-value model. This is not a claim that the actual printer was random or that the original coupon is recoverable by a stochastic model. It undercuts any unsupported argument that retry proximity establishes redundant copies of one underlying coupon.

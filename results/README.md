For **K04**, the incident-date logical ESC-N route maps to job `PS-261003-0417`. Its last acknowledged RECOVER attempt is attempt 2. An [exact exhaustive test of all 9,285,570,000 dispersed three-byte candidate changes](h17-k04-three-byte-bound.md) found zero original K04 commitment matches; together with earlier zero-to-two-byte coverage this establishes that any accepted component differs from the recorded XOR result at **at least four distinct byte positions**. The three-byte result and the separately reported [1,820,475,000 four-nibble exclusion](h17-k04-four-nibble-bound.md) have each now been reproduced in full with independently implemented XOR-delta enumerators, exact original-target NO_MATCH outcomes, and independently specified positive fixtures (Agent 3, 18 October; reproducible sources and outputs appended to those canonical reports). The [printer cohort and identity audit](h17-spool-cohort-identity-audit.md) additionally distinguishes 128 regular `+00:00` entries from five `Z`-timestamp recovery tail entries, and documents a reused job/attempt identifier.

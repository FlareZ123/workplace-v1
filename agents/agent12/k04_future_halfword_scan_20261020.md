## Original source and mechanism

- Original `resources/evidence/transfers/transfer-audit.jsonl`: for each transfer, retain the highest `attempt` per `seq` and join only **adjacent sequence pairs** `(0,1),(2,3),(4,5),(6,7)` of two-byte hexadecimal checkpoints. This tests all four original transfer groups, not only the accepted K07 chain.
- Original `resources/evidence/telemetry/freeze-export.jsonl`: `DIAG_WORD` records marked `GOOD` and `HOLD`, greatest `revision` per `tag,seq`, joined in the same adjacent sequence pairs as two big-endian unsigned 16-bit values. This keeps the fields' documented ordering while allowing the possibility that two successive halfwords serialize one 32-bit producer output.
- Total **20** original ordered four-byte pairings. For each, compare four explicitly stated byte layouts: unchanged, byte reversal within both halfwords, full word reversal, and swapped halfwords. This yields **80 oriented candidate tempered MT words**. Untemper with standard MT19937 transformation and test distinct ordered pairs against three previously independently source-derived future-state relations. No mixing arbitrary two-byte fragments from unrelated records.

The known original-spool/source-consistent `p=955` model predicts `X1412 XOR X1866 = BD2761F8` (all 32 bits), `(X1185 XOR X1412) & BFFFFFFF = 359EC0C7` (31 bits), and `(X1411 XOR X1865) & 9FFFFFFF = 0CDE27B9` (30 bits). See [original donor atlas](../../results/h17-k04-forward-donor-leverage-and-source-xor.md). The 955 layout is a GF(2)-compatible mathematical model but already conflicts with some stricter CLASS/DEST physical-producer assumptions.

## What this excludes and what it cannot prove

**Excluded:** these *specific*, documented pairwise concatenations and four serialization views, assigned in either ordered future-donor direction, as a pair satisfying any of the three above XOR relations in the tested p955 source-consistent model. This is a small source-motivated, new carrier class, not an exhaustive investigation of all compositions of halfwords.

**Not excluded:** other fields or transformations, an independently indexed future MT word that is not a contiguous `u16+u16` pair, a different source chronology, or a producer unrelated to MT19937. The record supplies no independent evidence that AX/telemetry halfwords were generated by the spool's PRNG, and source timestamps cannot be used as MT draw indices without a historical provenance argument. Thus this negative result does **not** constrain actual K04 target bits and offers no exact K08 language. **Actual progress: K04 0 newly recovered bits, K08 0 recovered words, REC8 acceptance incomplete.**
